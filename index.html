What We Do

Total Exterior Restoration

Every job goes through strict alignment, priming, colour-matching, and a dust-free low-bake oven cure — guaranteed to blend seamlessly with your factory finish.

🔨

Dents & Panel Beating

Complete extraction and reshape of structural metalwork to smooth factory contours after impacts or parking collisions.

✨

Scuffs & Key Scratches

Deep door abrasions, paint key damage, and multi-layer gouges seamlessly removed, primed, and colour-matched.

🎨

Low-Bake Oven Resprays

Enclosed spray booth ensuring dust-free paint distribution and a durable, high-bake clear coat cure — same setup as main dealers.

🛡️

Bumper & Mirror Repairs

Plastic welding and textured skin rebuilds for scuffed bumpers and mirror covers — avoiding expensive replacement costs.

⚡

Structural Welding

Heavy-duty patch alignment and clean metal joining repairs executed directly on premises, all grades of steel.

🪞

Polish & Paint Correction

Full exterior surface correction to eliminate swirl marks and oxidation — restoring a deep showroom gloss.

About

Meet Josh.

Josh has been working on cars his whole life. Bexleyheath Car Body Repairs is a family-run workshop tucked behind The Broadway in DA6 — not a franchise, not a chain. Just a highly skilled tradesperson with the right equipment and the right attitude.

The shop runs a dedicated low-bake spray oven booth — the same professional-grade setup used by main dealers. Colour blends are seamless, finishes last, and Josh handles every job personally.

Insurance jobs, private repairs, full resprays — every car treated like his own.
